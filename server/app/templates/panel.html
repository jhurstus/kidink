Comic panel playground

Reusable Ben-Day halftone shading and hand-drawn comic borders, built from comic.css + the comic_panel() Jinja macro. Drag the sliders — or type exact values into the number boxes — to dial in the live panel below, flip on a second halftone to layer two fields, and resize the panel itself. Everything two-way-syncs with the editable Macro invocation block: tweak a control and the macro updates; edit the macro and the controls + preview follow. The gallery shows the parameter range and multi-halftone layering.
